MotionFreela Strategic Pricing & Retention Analysis 🎥
An interactive decision-support tool developed to optimize pricing structures and improve customer retention for a subscription-based video production company.

🔍 Strategic Insights Captured
Subscription Performance: Tracked critical metrics, including an Average Monthly Price of $2.63K and a 0.31 Conversion Rate across all tiers.

Churn & Retention Analysis: Identified churn patterns across subscription plans (Starter, Enterprise, Pro, Growth), correlating lower churn with higher-tier "Growth" plans.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV): Calculated a total CLV of $17.64K, providing a benchmark for sustainable scaling and acquisition costs.

Industry-Specific Demand: Visualized preferred video types (Ad Creative, Explainer, Reels, etc.) across Education, Retail, Tech, Healthcare, and Finance sectors to guide targeted marketing.

Feature Request Prioritization: Analyzed common requests like Faster Turnaround and Custom Templates across customer types (Agency, SMB, Startup) to refine service offerings.

🛠️ Dashboard Logic & Features
Interactive Simulation: Users can filter results by Industry, Customer Type, and Video Length to simulate specific market scenarios.

Engagement Scoring: Integrated an Engagement Score treemap to visualize which segments are most active within the platform.

Executive Summary & Strategy Exploration: Structured with dedicated views for high-level KPIs and deep-dive strategic analysis.
